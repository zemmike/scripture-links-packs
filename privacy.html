Terms of use

Scripture Links is a free app for reading and studying the Bible. You may use it for personal study, teaching and conversation.

The answers, topics and connections were compiled with care from Holy Scripture and the sources named in them. They have not been reviewed by the clergy of any church and are not the official teaching of any church. Where an answer says it has not been reviewed, please read the verses and sources for yourself and ask your priest or pastor.

The app links to websites of others (for example the pages of the quoted sources and archive.org). Their content and terms are their own.

The app is provided as it is, without any warranty. To the extent the law allows, the developer is not liable for damage from its use. Liability for intent and gross negligence, and under mandatory law, remains unaffected.

These terms may change with new versions of the app; the date above shows the current version.

Privacy

The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect or send personal data to the developer.

Everything you create (bookmarks, highlights, notes, memory verses, reading plans, saved answers and settings) is stored only on your phone. Backups are files that you save or share yourself.

The app uses the internet only when you ask for it: to download packs (from GitHub, which receives your IP address like any website; see GitHub’s privacy statement), and to open a source in your browser (the website you open receives your request).

Reminders are created on the phone; nothing is sent. “Report a problem” opens your share menu and sends only what you choose, through the app you choose.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app’s data in your phone’s backup to your Google account, under Google’s terms.

Contact for privacy questions: zemiketube@gmail.com

Datenschutz

Die App hat kein Konto, keine Werbung, keine Analyse und kein Tracking. Sie erhebt keine personenbezogenen Daten und sendet nichts an den Entwickler.

Alles, was du anlegst (Lesezeichen, Markierungen, Notizen, Merkverse, Lesepläne, gespeicherte Antworten und Einstellungen), bleibt nur auf deinem Telefon. Sicherungen sind Dateien, die du selbst speicherst oder teilst.

Die App nutzt das Internet nur, wenn du es auslöst: zum Herunterladen von Paketen (von GitHub, das wie jede Website deine IP-Adresse erhält; siehe die Datenschutzerklärung von GitHub) und zum Öffnen einer Quelle im Browser (die geöffnete Website erhält deine Anfrage).

Erinnerungen werden auf dem Telefon erzeugt; nichts wird gesendet. „Problem melden“ öffnet das Teilen-Menü und sendet nur, was du auswählst, über die App, die du wählst.

Ist die Android-Sicherung eingeschaltet, kann Android die Daten der App in die Sicherung deines Telefons bei deinem Google-Konto aufnehmen, nach den Bedingungen von Google.
